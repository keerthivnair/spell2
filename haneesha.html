Cities in India

  1. AndhraPradesh
    • Vijayawada
      • A major commercial hub and the proposed legislative capital of Andhra Pradesh, known for its infrastructure and connectivity.
    • Visakhapatnam
      • A key port city and industrial center, also famous for its beaches and the Eastern Naval Command.
    • Tirupati
      • A major pilgrimage city, home to the famous Tirumala Venkateswara Temple, attracting millions of devotees annually.
  2. Tamil Nadu
    • Chennai
      • The capital city, known for its IT industry, port, and cultural heritage; it's a major economic and educational hub.
    • Coimbatore
      • An industrial city known as the "Manchester of South India" for its textile industry and engineering firms.
    • Madurai
      • One of the oldest cities in India, famous for the Meenakshi Amman Temple and its rich cultural and historical significance.
  3. Karnataka
    • Bengaluru
      • Known as the "Silicon Valley of India," it's the leading IT hub of the country. It also has a vibrant startup ecosystem and modern infrastructure.
    • Mysuru
      • A cultural capital famous for the Mysore Palace and Dasara festival. It's also known for its yoga centers, silk, and sandalwood products.
    • Mangaluru
      • A major port city and commercial center on the west coast. It's important for trade, education, and diverse industries like petrochemicals and fisheries.
  4. Maharashtra
    • Mumbai
      • The financial capital of India and home to Bollywood, India’s largest film industry,It's also a major port city with top corporate headquarters and global connectivity.
    • Pune
      • Known as the "Oxford of the East" due to its prestigious educational institutions,It's also a growing IT and automobile manufacturing hub.
    • Nagpur
      • Known as the "Orange City" and a major center for orange trade in India,It’s also the geographical center of India and an important political and logistics hub.
  5. Uttar Pradesh
    • Agra
      • Home to the iconic Taj Mahal, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and major tourist attraction,Agra is also known for its leather industry and historical Mughal architecture.
    • Varanasi
      • One of the world’s oldest living cities, it holds immense religious and cultural importance,It's a major center for learning, spirituality, and silk weaving.
    • Lucknow
      • The capital city of Uttar Pradesh, known for its rich Nawabi heritage, culture, and cuisine,It's a major center for administration, education, and handicrafts like chikankari embroidery.
  6. West Bengal
    • Durgapur
      • Known as the "Steel City of East India", it houses one of India’s largest steel plants,It's an important industrial city with power plants, educational institutions, and a growing IT sector.
    • Kolkata
      • Once the capital of British India, it remains a cultural and intellectual hub,Known for its colonial architecture, arts, literature, and festivals like Durga Puja.
    • Siliguri
      • Known as the "Gateway to Northeast India," it connects the northeastern states with the rest of the country,It’s a key center for tea trade, tourism, and transportation, located near the foothills of the Himalayas.
  7. Telangana
    • Hyderabad
      • The capital city and major IT hub of India, known for its tech industry and historical sites like Charminar and Golconda Fort.
    • Warangal
      • Famous for its rich Kakatiya dynasty heritage, including the Warangal Fort and Thousand Pillar Temple; a growing educational and cultural center.
    • Nizamabad
      • An important agricultural and commercial city, known for its turmeric production and historical landmarks like Nizamabad Fort.
